## Style Prompt

Technical classroom explainer for speech emotion recognition. The visual style should feel like a modern AI course slide deck: dark canvas, clear data-flow diagrams, waveform and neural-network motifs, calm but energetic motion. Prioritize readability for classroom projection.

## Colors

- Background: `#07111f`
- Panel: `#10243a`
- Accent cyan: `#38d5ff`
- Accent amber: `#ffbf5f`
- Success green: `#65e6a4`
- Text: `#eef6ff`

## Typography

- Chinese UI/title: Microsoft YaHei, Noto Sans SC, sans-serif
- Code/labels: JetBrains Mono, Consolas, monospace

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use a generic blue gradient-only background.
- Do not place dense paragraphs on screen.
- Do not use tiny code text that students cannot read from the back of a classroom.
- Do not rely on decorative shapes without explaining the data/model flow.
